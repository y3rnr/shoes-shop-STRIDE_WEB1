OUR PHILOSOPHY

Make room for your feet.

Why should your shoes decide the shape of your feet? We love a wider toe box, a flexible feel and footwear made for everyday movement.

“Good shoes should follow your feet, not fight them.”

WHY WE LIKE A WIDER SHAPE

Did you know? Your toes need space.

Some shoes taper sharply at the front. Barefoot-inspired designs give the forefoot more room and often use flatter, more flexible soles. The best fit is the one that feels comfortable for you.

01

Room for your toes

A wider front lets your toes spread without being squeezed together.

02

Closer to the ground

A low heel-to-toe profile offers a different feel underfoot.

03

Freedom to flex

Flexible soles let the shoe move more easily with your foot.

New to minimalist shoes? Start gradually, especially for running. Different activities and feet need different levels of support.
